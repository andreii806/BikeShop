## What I learned / what did not work
- Vite serves the app on localhost; the page can no longer be opened by double-clicking the file.
- JSX differences from HTML: `className`, closed tags, one root element or a fragment.
- `node_modules/` and `dist/` must stay out of Git; `npm run build` checks that the project builds.
